⚑ Craft decisions (Emil)

  • Restraint by frequency: a sidebar is opened 100×/day → hovers are opacity-only, ≤150ms, zero bounce. No springs.
  • Never scale(0): delete/flip handles enter from scale(0.9)+opacity.
  • Blur-masks the crossfade: the flip overlaps two text states — filter:blur(3px) blends them into one motion.
  • Custom curves: built-ins too weak → cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1).
  • GPU-only: every animation is transform/opacity/filter; grid-rows is the one layout exception (occasional, cheap here).
  • Reduced-motion: movement removed, opacity kept — toggle your OS setting to verify.
  • Hover-gated: all :hover behind @media (hover:hover) so touch taps don't false-fire.
